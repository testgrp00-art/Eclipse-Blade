ABOUT THE GAME

TAMOVEER — MOBILE & DESKTOP
Original offline 2D dark fantasy action game. Three heroes, 300 HP survival mode with permanent death at 0 HP, endlessly paced levels until death, recurring boss battles every fourth level, shadow enemies and combos. High-resolution procedural artwork and fast continuous combat.

TAMOVEER · NITAI STUDIO · LAST UPDATED 23 OCTOBER 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tamoveer (“the Game”), developed by Nitai Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. The Game has no account, login, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it makes no network requests to send your data anywhere.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves only the following on your own device, using your browser’s local storage: your settings (sound, screen shake, damage numbers, mouse aiming, graphics quality) and your best score. This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing the site data or uninstalling the app.

3. Offline use and caching

When installed as an app, the Game stores its own files on your device so it can run offline.

4. Permissions

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files. It may use the screen orientation and fullscreen features of your device purely to display the Game in landscape.

The Game contains no third-party SDKs or ads. If you open the Game through a website, app store or host, that platform may collect its own standard technical information under its own privacy policy.

6. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains fantasy action violence; parents and guardians should judge suitability.
